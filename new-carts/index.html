CROWNED

You are the crown. Whatever you land on, you become.

A tiny gold crown with two eyes has a mountain to climb and no legs to climb it with. So it borrows some. Land on a creature's head and you are that creature, with everything it can do.

  • Wear the frogIts tongue catches the hanging rings and swings you across the marsh.
  • Wear the ramLower your head and charge.
  • Eight kingdoms, one mountainClimb from the burrows at the bottom to the nest at the top.
  • Dethrone the Heron KingKnock off his crown. Then wear the king.
  • Platformer
  • 1 or 2 players
  • Concept

ONE FLAME

Two children. One fire. A whole ice age to cross.

The tribe's last fire is a small torch, and it is the only light there is. In the caves you can see the ice near the flame and nothing else.

  • One carries, one guardsThe light in one pair of hands, a spear in the other.
  • Throw the flameWhen the gap is too wide to cross together, the fire goes first.
  • Feed itThe flame burns down as you travel. Wood keeps it alive.
  • Pick your routeDay 3, the Frozen Pass: two ways through, and the choice is yours.
  • Adventure
  • Alone or together
  • Concept

Simulated screens.

SCRIBBLE BEAST

Draw your own monster. Raise it from pocket-size to mountain-size.

You draw the beast yourself, one pixel at a time. It starts out small enough for a pocket and grows into the monster you drew.

  • Every crayon is a statRed for power, blue for speed, green for toughness.
  • Snap on the partsLegs, arms and a tail are what let it walk, swim and smash.
  • Ride itOnce it is big enough to carry you. Mind the erasers: they rub out the ground.
  • Live on its backBy day 41 it is taller than the mountains, and there is a whole camp up there.
  • Draw and platform
  • Concept

Simulated screens.

BOLTS

Every opponent is a giant robot. And the robot is the level.

A girl mechanic, a wrench, and a walking machine the size of a building. The fight starts before you move, because the robot has already scouted you.

  • It learns your habitsClimb left too often and it guards the left leg.
  • Read the tellDodge. Grab on.
  • Pull the boltsClimb the robot and take it apart, eight bolts at a time.
  • First up: Boiler BobSix stories. Ninety tons.
  • Action
  • Solo or versus
  • Concept

Simulated screens.

DROP & DASH

One player runs. The other drops the ground.

A small astronaut has somewhere to be and nothing to stand on. A cargo drone fixes that, three blocks at a time.

  • Blocks become groundThe moment they land, the runner can stand on them.
  • Clear a rowWall to wall, and it pays out in stars.
  • Bury the beastBosses are not fought. They are buried.
  • Three ways to playSolo, team up, or versus.
  • Puzzle platformer
  • 1 or 2 players
